## Location
A hidden valley approximately 3 hours walk northeast of Wave Echo Cave, concealed by ancient illusions and natural rock formations. The valley doesn't appear on any modern maps - only those who know what to look for can find it.

## Discovery
The dwarves arguing outside Wave Echo Cave are actually lost - they're looking for the Sundered Valley but can't agree on the directions. The kobolds in the cave have a partial map showing "Very Dangerous Valley - Do Not Safety Check!"

## Appearance
- **The Approach**: A narrow ravine that seems to dead-end against solid rock. The illusion parts for those who speak either prophet's name.
- **The Valley**: A perfect oval depression, 300 feet across, with naturally terraced sides forming amphitheater seating
- **The Center**: A circular plaza of fused glass where reality was wounded - it shimmers with impossible colors
- **The Camping Grounds**: Natural ledges and caves dot the valley walls where delegations set up camps

## Features
### Reality Scars
- Gravity occasionally hiccups - small objects float for seconds
- Time moves strangely - shadows don't match the sun
- Whispers in languages that were never spoken echo randomly
- The temperature is always perfectly comfortable, regardless of season

### The Speaking Stones
Seven pillars around the valley's edge, each inscribed in a different language:
- Dwarvish: "Here Order and Chaos met"
- Elvish: "Here they nearly destroyed all"
- Draconic: "Here they learned balance"
- Common: "Here they must choose again"
- Giant: "The mountain remembers"
- Celestial: "The prophets watch"
- Primordial: "The Heart decides"

### The Manifestation Point
The glass circle at center is where the Heart emerges. Currently it's empty except for:
- Faint warmth rising from below
- Occasional brief visions of the past war
- A sense of anticipation, like held breath

## Why It's Perfect for the Contest
- **Isolated**: Can't accidentally stumble upon it
- **Symbolic**: The actual site of the original Sundering
- **Practical**: Natural amphitheater for delegations to gather
- **Flexible**: Can be dramatic or comedic as needed
- **Non-disruptive**: Completely separate from Phandalin and main campaign

## Current State (When Party Arrives)
Depending on which encounter:
- **Dwarven Encounter**: They're trying to find it, have conflicting maps
- **Kobold Encounter**: They have a "danger map" marking it
- Early delegations have started arriving and setting up camps
- Smoke from various cooking fires rises from the valley
- Sounds of argument, laughter, and preparation echo off the walls

## Daran's Connection
Daran knows exactly where it is but hasn't mentioned it. If asked:
- "The Valley? Oh, you mean where it all happens."
- "Follow the old marker stones north from the cave. Look for where shadows fall upward."
- "You'll know you're close when your compass spins."

## Navigation Clues
For the party to find it (or help others find it):
- **From Wave Echo Cave**: Follow dried riverbed north, turn east at the standing stones
- **Marker Stones**: Ancient stones every half-mile, each slightly warm to touch
- **Natural Signs**: Birds fly in circles overhead, refusing to cross the valley
- **Magical Detection**: Any detect magic shows a massive aura ahead
- **The Test**: Must speak "Serenity," "Strife," or "Balance" to pass the illusion

## Delegation Camps (When Party Arrives)
Around the valley rim:
- **North**: Githyanki (silver tents, utterly silent)
- **South**: Mixed dwarven camps (lots of shouting)
- **East**: Nature delegations (trees literally walking around)
- **West**: Kobolds (extensive "safety" preparations)
- **Scattered**: Merchant wagons, curious travelers, confused locals

The valley provides perfect separation from the main campaign while maintaining connection to the adventure!
