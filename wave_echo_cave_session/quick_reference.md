## Dwarven Encounter - Quick Flow

### Scene Setup
- 20 dwarves in circle watching ritual combat
- Grimvar (Order) vs Thorina (Chaos), tied 4-4
- Dhora refereeing, exhausted

### Key Decision Points
1. **Initial Approach**
   - Party can champion either side
   - Try to stop/mediate
   - Watch and learn

2. **Philosophical Debate** (Skill Challenge)
   - Round 1: What does Wave Echo Cave teach? (DC 13)
   - Round 2: What threatens us more? (DC 14)
   - Round 3: What tell our children? (DC 15)

3. **Hobgoblin Attack** (Combat)
   - Captain + 6 warriors + 2 worgs
   - Target: Wagon (50 HP, AC 15)
   - Dwarves need coordination or fail

4. **Resolution**
   - Unite through balance
   - Respectful division
   - One side dominates

### Quick NPC References
- **Grimvar**: "The mountain does not bend!"
- **Thorina**: "Tradition is peer pressure from dead people!"
- **Dhora**: "Just... make it stop..."

## Kobold Encounter - Quick Flow

### Scene Setup
- 18 kobolds trapped by magical barriers
- 5 sections with different crises
- Yip coordinating (badly) from center

### Crisis Sections
1. **Flood**: 4 kobolds, water rising (1 ft/round)
2. **Jellies**: 3 kobolds, 2 ochre jellies
3. **Walls**: 4 kobolds, closing walls (5 ft/round)
4. **Riddles**: 5 kobolds, lightning for wrong answers
5. **Control**: 2 kobolds pressing random buttons

### Control Panel Solution
- 6 buttons (Hammer, Lightning, Water, Fire, Eye, Hand)
- Password in 3 parts (Dwarvish, Elvish, Draconic)
- Overload option: All buttons at once (DC 18)

### Shield Guardian
- Activates at 50% kobolds saved
- 142 HP, AC 17, regenerates near panel
- Prioritizes resetting traps
- Can be deactivated with password

### Resolution Paths
- **Order**: Systematic rescue = Order kobolds
- **Chaos**: Creative chaos = Chaos kobolds
- **Balance**: Both methods = Undecided kobolds

### Quick Kobold Names
Skrik, Pip, Tik-Tak, Yeep, Grik, Zap, Mip, Thinky
