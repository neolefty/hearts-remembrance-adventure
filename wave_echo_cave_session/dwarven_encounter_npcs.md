# Dwarven Encounter NPCs

## Elder Grimvar Ironfoot
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), lawful neutral*
**Champion of Order**

**Armor Class** 16 (chain mail, shield)  
**Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Con +4, Wis +4  
**Skills** History +3, Insight +4, Persuasion +3  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

**Dwarven Resilience.** Grimvar has advantage on saving throws against poison.

**Traditional Stance.** When Grimvar takes the Dodge action, allies within 10 feet gain +1 AC until his next turn.

**Voice of the Ancestors.** Once per day, Grimvar can invoke ancestral wisdom, giving himself advantage on Persuasion checks for 10 minutes.

### Actions
**Multiattack.** Grimvar makes two attacks with his ancestral hammer.

**Ancestral Hammer.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If fighting in ritual combat, deals nonlethal damage.

**Shield Bash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage and target must make DC 13 Strength save or be knocked prone.

### Reactions
**Defensive Master.** When hit by an attack, Grimvar can use his reaction to reduce damage by 1d8 + 3.

### Personality
- Quotes ancient dwarven proverbs constantly
- Respectful but stubborn
- Values honor above victory
- **Quote:** "The mountain does not bend, and neither shall we."

## Master Artificer Thorina Goldspeak
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), chaotic good*
**Champion of Chaos**

**Armor Class** 15 (studded leather, shield)  
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Con +4, Int +5  
**Skills** Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Persuasion +3, Tinker's Tools +7  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish, Gnomish  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

**Dwarven Resilience.** Thorina has advantage on saving throws against poison.

**Innovative Fighter.** Thorina can use Intelligence instead of Strength for attack and damage rolls.

**Experimental Devices.** Thorina has 3 experimental devices per day (see below).

### Actions
**Multiattack.** Thorina makes two attacks with her spring-loaded gauntlets.

**Spring-Loaded Gauntlets.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage. Can knock target back 5 feet on critical hit.

**Experimental Device (3/Day).** Choose one:
- *Smoke Bomb:* 20-foot radius heavily obscured area for 1 minute
- *Thunder Crystal:* All creatures within 10 feet make DC 13 Con save or be deafened for 1 minute
- *Spring Boots:* Jump 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks

### Bonus Actions
**Quick Innovation.** Thorina can use Tinker's Tools to create a minor helpful device (DM discretion).

### Personality
- Enthusiastic about new ideas
- Impatient with "because we've always done it that way"
- Singed eyebrows from experiments
- **Quote:** "Tradition is just peer pressure from dead people!"

## Veteran Dhora Greybeard
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), lawful neutral*
**Neutral Referee**

**Armor Class** 14 (studded leather)  
**Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Con +3, Wis +5  
**Skills** Insight +5, History +3, Persuasion +4, Performance +4  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish, Elvish  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)

**Dwarven Resilience.** Dhora has advantage on saving throws against poison.

**Voice of Authority.** Creatures must make DC 13 Wisdom save to attack Dhora while she's serving as referee.

**Impartial Judge.** Dhora has advantage on Insight checks to detect lies or determine motivations.

### Actions
**Ceremonial Staff.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

**Call for Order.** All creatures within 30 feet must make DC 13 Wisdom save or be unable to attack for 1 turn (recharge 5-6).

**Invoke Thuul-Kadar.** Dhora can declare ritual combat rules, giving both combatants +2 to AC but restricting them to nonlethal damage.

### Personality
- Exhausted by the constant arguing
- Deeply respects both tradition and innovation
- Just wants the clan to stay together
- **Quote:** "The only thing worse than change is refusing to change. The only thing worse than that is arguing about it for three days straight."

## Dwarven Delegation Members
*Medium humanoid (mountain dwarf), various alignments*

**Armor Class** 13 (leather armor)  
**Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** One craft skill +4  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

### Actions
**Warhammer.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

**Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8) piercing damage.

### Variations
- **Order Supporters:** +2 to AC when adjacent to ally
- **Chaos Supporters:** +5 ft. movement, advantage on initiative
- **Neutral/Tired:** Disadvantage on all checks due to exhaustion
- **Young Apprentices:** Only 13 HP but eager to prove themselves
