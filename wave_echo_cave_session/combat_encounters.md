# Combat Encounter Stat Blocks

## Hobgoblin Raid (Dwarven Encounter)

### Hobgoblin Captain
*Medium humanoid (goblinoid), lawful evil*

**Armor Class** 17 (half plate)  
**Hit Points** 39 (6d8 + 12)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) |

**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common, Goblin  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

**Martial Advantage.** Once per turn, the hobgoblin can deal an extra 10 (3d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 feet of an ally of the hobgoblin that isn't incapacitated.

### Actions
**Multiattack.** The hobgoblin makes two greatsword attacks or two javelin attacks.

**Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) slashing damage.

**Javelin.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** For 1 minute, the hobgoblin can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature that it can see within 30 feet makes an attack roll or saving throw. The creature can add a d4 to its roll provided it can hear and understand the hobgoblin.

### Tactics
- Focuses on wagon/supplies first
- Uses Leadership to coordinate attacks
- Retreats if reduced to 10 HP

## Shield Guardian (Kobold Encounter)

### Ancient Shield Guardian
*Large construct, unaligned*
**Forge of Spells Defense System**

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 142 (15d10 + 60)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 8 (-1) | 18 (+4) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 10 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages** understands Dwarvish and Elvish but can't speak  
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

**Bound to Control Panel.** The guardian returns to the control panel to repair itself, regaining 10 HP at the start of its turn if it's within 10 feet of the panel.

**Containment Protocol.** The guardian prioritizes resetting traps over attacking. On its turn, if any trap is disabled, it must use its action to reactivate one trap if within 30 feet of a control mechanism.

**Spell Storing.** The guardian stores one spell of 4th level or lower. Currently stores *Lightning Bolt* (8d6 lightning damage, DC 15 Dex save).

### Actions
**Multiattack.** The guardian makes two fist attacks.

**Fist.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

**Reset Protocol (Recharge 5-6).** The guardian activates all traps within 60 feet. Each trap immediately triggers its effect.

### Reactions
**Shield.** When a creature makes an attack against a trap or control panel within 5 feet of the guardian, the guardian grants +5 AC to that object.

### Legendary Actions (2 per round)
The guardian can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from options below:
- **Move.** The guardian moves up to its speed
- **Repair.** The guardian repairs a trap within 5 feet
- **Pulse.** All creatures within 10 feet make DC 15 Strength save or be pushed back 10 feet

### Defeating the Guardian
**Combat:** Reduce to 0 HP (difficult with regeneration)
**Deactivation:** Three successful DC 15 checks at control panel while guardian is distracted
**Password:** Speaking the three-part password causes guardian to stand down
**Overload:** Pressing all buttons simultaneously (DC 18 Investigation to figure out)

## Environmental Hazards (Kobold Encounter)

### Flooding Chamber
**Trigger:** Already activated  
**Effect:** Water rises 1 foot per round  
**Solution:** 
- Valve puzzle (DC 15 Investigation)
- Strength DC 18 to force drainage
- Freeze water with magic

### Crushing Walls
**Trigger:** Already activated  
**Effect:** Walls close 5 feet per round  
**Current Gap:** 20 feet  
**Solution:**
- DC 20 Strength to jam mechanism
- Disable gears (DC 15 Thieves' Tools)
- Stone Shape or similar magic

### Lightning Riddles
**Trigger:** Wrong answer to riddle  
**Effect:** 3d6 lightning damage (DC 13 Dex save for half)  
**Riddles:** All about Order vs Chaos philosophy  
**Solution:**
- Answer correctly
- Disable with DC 17 Thieves' Tools
- Absorb lightning with metal/magic
