# Kobold Encounter NPCs

## Assistant Trapmaster Yip
*Small humanoid (kobold), lawful chaotic (yes, both)*
**Leader of the Safety Survey Team**

**Armor Class** 14 (leather armor, high Dex)  
**Hit Points** 27 (6d6 + 6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 7 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Int +4  
**Skills** Investigation +4, Perception +2, Thieves' Tools +5, Performance +3  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

**Pack Tactics.** Yip has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of Yip's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and isn't incapacitated.

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** While in sunlight, Yip has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

**Safety Expertise.** Yip has advantage on checks to detect traps and hazards (even ones that don't exist).

**Nervous Leader.** When Yip takes damage, all kobolds within 30 feet must make DC 10 Wisdom save or spend their next action running in a random direction.

### Actions
**Safety Whistle.** All kobolds within 60 feet gain advantage on their next saving throw (recharge 5-6).

**Clipboard Attack.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and target must make DC 12 Wisdom save or be confused by safety regulations for 1 turn.

**Emergency Flare.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30 ft., creates bright light in 20-foot radius for 1 minute. Creatures in area must make DC 12 Con save or be blinded for 1 round.

### Reactions
**Safety First!** When an ally within 30 feet is hit by an attack, Yip can shout a warning, granting them +2 AC against that attack.

### Personality
- Takes copious notes about everything
- Genuinely wants to help but makes things worse
- Speaks in safety jargon nobody understands
- **Quote:** "According to paragraph 7, subsection 3 of the safety manual... wait, which manual was that?"

### Yip's Safety Equipment
- Clipboard with incomprehensible diagrams
- 17 different whistles
- Hard hat with 6 lit candles
- "DANGER" and "SAFETY" signs (uses interchangeably)
- Map labeled "VERY UNSAFE AREAS" (it's just random scribbles)

## Kobold Safety Surveyors
*Small humanoid (kobold), chaotic neutral*
**The Survey Team (17 kobolds)**

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 5 (2d6 - 2)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 7 (-2) | 15 (+2) | 9 (-1) | 8 (-1) | 8 (-1) | 8 (-1) |

**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9  
**Languages** Draconic, broken Common  
**Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP)

**Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls with ally within 5 feet of target.

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception in sunlight.

### Actions
**Safety Implement.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) damage. (Random implement: clipboard, safety cone, "danger" sign, etc.)

**Sling.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

### Special Team Roles (Choose 1 per kobold)

**Safety Inspector** (4 kobolds)
- Constantly writing notes
- Can detect traps (50% accuracy)
- Shouts "UNSAFE!" giving disadvantage on stealth

**Emergency Response** (4 kobolds)
- Carries water bucket (always spills it)
- Advantage on Dexterity saves
- Panics easily (WIS save DC 10 or flee when damaged)

**Trap Analyst** (3 kobolds)
- +2 to Investigation
- Triggers traps "to study them"
- Proudly shows scars from "safety research"

**Philosophy Debater** (3 kobolds)
- Argues about Order vs Chaos constantly
- Disadvantage on all checks (too distracted)
- Can actually explain the Contest (poorly)

**Documentation Specialist** (3 kobolds)
- Draws "helpful" diagrams on walls
- Carries chalk and charcoal
- Maps are always wrong but confident

### Group Behaviors
- When 3+ kobolds together: Gain confidence, act braver
- When alone: Immediately seek other kobolds
- When Yip is hurt: 50% chance to flee, 50% chance to swarm attacker
- When arguing philosophy: Completely ignore danger

### Sample Names & Personalities
1. **Skrik** - Believes everything is trapped
2. **Pip** - Counts everything obsessively  
3. **Tik-Tak** - Speaks only in safety slogans
4. **Yeep** - Terrified of shadows (including own)
5. **Grik** - Thinks Order means standing still
6. **Zap** - Thinks Chaos means random actions
7. **Mip** - Carries 47 "safety chickens" (regular chickens)

## Special Kobold NPCs

### Kobold Philosopher
*Small humanoid (kobold), true neutral*
**Trapped in the Philosophy Circle**

**Armor Class** 12  
**Hit Points** 7 (2d6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

**Languages** Common, Draconic, Philosophical Nonsense  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

**Deep Thoughts.** Has advantage on Intelligence checks but disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks (too distracted by thinking).

### Actions
**Philosophical Argument.** Target must make DC 11 Wisdom save or be stunned for 1 round while considering the nature of existence.

### Personality
- Named **Thinky**
- Only kobold who actually understands the Contest
- Explains everything as a metaphor
- **Quote:** "Is trap? Or is trap metaphor for life choices? Both unsafe!"

## Kobold Safety Equipment Table (d12)
When kobolds need equipment, roll:

1. Hard hat with lit candles (provides light, fire hazard)
2. "Safety rope" (tangled mess)
3. Warning whistle (ear-piercing)
4. Bucket of "safety water" (usually empty)
5. "Danger" sign (uses as shield)
6. Clipboard with "notes" (child-like drawings)
7. Safety manual (in Draconic, upside down)
8. Emergency flare (sets things on fire)
9. First aid kit (contains only bandages and chicken feathers)
10. Safety cone (worn as hat)
11. "Anti-trap spray" (water in bottle)
12. Map of safe routes (leads directly into danger)
