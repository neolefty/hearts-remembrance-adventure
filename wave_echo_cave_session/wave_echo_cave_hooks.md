# Wave Echo Cave - Contest Hooks (Full Session Version)

## Option 1: Exiting the Cave - The Dwarven Philosophical Crisis

### The Setup
As the party exits Wave Echo Cave, they find a dwarven caravan stopped on the path. A circle of 20 dwarves surrounds two combatants locked in ritualistic combat, while a elder dwarf referee counts slowly. The wagon bears Clan Battlehammer insignia, but the group is clearly divided.

### The Scene
**The Ritual Combat:**
- **Elder Grimvar Ironfoot** (Order) - Fighting with ancient ceremonial hammer, defensive style
- **Thorina Goldspeak** (Chaos) - Using experimental spring-loaded gauntlets, aggressive style
- They're bloodied but not trying to kill each other
- This is **Thuul-Kadar** - an ancient dwarven rite to settle philosophical disputes

**The Referee**: **Veteran Dhora Greybeard** (neutral)
- "Point to Tradition! Defense holds!"
- "Point to Innovation! Clever strike!"
- Currently tied at 4-4, first to 7 wins

### The Deeper Conflict
**When party approaches, Dhora calls a rest:**
- "Strangers! You arrive at a moment of destiny. Clan Battlehammer must choose its path for the Contest."
- "These two represent our divide. But Thuul-Kadar can be satisfied by champions..."
- Both fighters are exhausted; party could substitute or influence outcome

### The Stakes
**Grimvar's Position (Order):**
- "The Forge of Spells in that cave proves our ancestors' wisdom!"
- "We must preserve what works, honor the traditions"
- "At the Contest, we'll argue for the stability that builds civilizations"
- Shows party an ancient runestone: "This has guided us for 1,000 years"

**Thorina's Position (Chaos):**
- "The Forge was an EXPERIMENT! Our ancestors were innovators!"
- "Tradition becomes stagnation. We must evolve or die"
- "At the Contest, we'll argue for the freedom that creates progress"
- Shows party her mechanical map: "This shows seventeen new ore veins tradition would never find"

### The Challenge Layer 1: The Combat (Optional)
If party wants to champion either side:
- **Combat Rules**: First to 7 touches wins
- Use nonlethal damage only
- DC 12 Constitution saves against exhaustion every 3 rounds
- Crowd reactions affect combat (advantage/disadvantage based on impressive moves)

**If Party Refuses Combat:**
- Move to philosophical challenge instead
- Dwarves respect this as "wisdom over force"

### The Challenge Layer 2: The Philosophical Debate
**Three Rounds of Arguments** (Skill Challenge):
**Round 1**: "What does Wave Echo Cave teach us?"
- Party must argue what they learned inside
- History checks about the Forge of Spells
- Persuasion to frame it as Order or Chaos
- Insight to understand what dwarves need to hear

**Round 2**: "What threatens our people more?"
- Order argues: "Chaos brought the orcs, the drow raids, the collapses"
- Chaos argues: "Stagnation made us weak, unable to adapt to threats"
- Party can introduce new perspectives

**Round 3**: "What should we tell our children?"
- This gets emotional - both sides have young apprentices watching
- Intimidation won't work here; only Persuasion or Performance
- Best argument about the future wins

### The Complication: External Threat
**Midway through debate/combat:**
**Hobgoblin Raid!** (Drawn by the noise)
- **Hobgoblin Captain** + 6 **Hobgoblins** + 2 **Worgs**
- They attack the wagon specifically (supplies and pay chest)
- Dwarves must work together or lose everything

**The Test**: 
- Grimvar wants defensive formation (Order)
- Thorina wants countercharge (Chaos)
- Party must help coordinate or both fail

**Combat Encounter**:
- Hobgoblins use tactics, focus fire
- Worgs try to scatter the dwarves
- Wagon has 50 HP, AC 15 - if destroyed, major problem
- Some dwarves are noncombatants (smiths, traders)

### The Resolution: Finding Synthesis
**After the battle**, both leaders are forced to acknowledge:
- Grimvar's defensive formation saved lives
- Thorina's aggressive tactics drove enemies off
- Both were necessary

**Party can guide conclusion:**
1. **Unity Through Balance**: Convince them to present BOTH arguments at Contest
2. **Respectful Division**: Two delegations, but as allies not enemies
3. **One Side Wins**: But winner acknowledges other's wisdom
4. **Create New Position**: "Dynamic Tradition" - honoring past while adapting

### Rewards & Consequences
**If United:**
- Gift: **Battlehammer Seal** (advantage on Persuasion with dwarves)
- Thorina offers experimental item
- Grimvar shares historical knowledge
- Strong unified delegation at Contest

**If Divided but Peaceful:**
- Smaller gifts from both sides
- Two delegations with different arguments
- Party might be asked to mediate again later

**If Hostile Division:**
- No gifts
- Dwarves may work against party later
- Weak presence at Contest

### Information Learned
- Full explanation of the Contest
- Order currently favored (world is chaotic)
- Other delegations gathering in Phandalin
- Twin Prophets have been seen
- Dragon delegation still missing

## Option 2: Staying in the Cave - The Kobold Containment Crisis

### The Setup
Deeper in Wave Echo Cave, party hears shrieking, explosions, and the sound of dozens of voices. They find **18 kobolds** who've accidentally triggered an ancient magical containment system while "safety inspecting."

### The Scene
**The Main Chamber:**
- Glowing magical barriers have trapped kobolds in different sections
- **Assistant Trapmaster Yip** coordinates from center platform
- Some kobolds trapped with remaining cave monsters
- Others trapped in rapidly flooding sections
- Barriers pulse and shift every few minutes

**The Five Sections** (each needs different solution):

**Section 1: The Flood Chamber**
- 4 kobolds standing on increasingly smaller dry spots
- Water rising from triggered trap
- Need to solve water flow puzzle or find drain
- Kobolds arguing whether drowning is Order (inevitable) or Chaos (random)

**Section 2: The Monster Pen**
- 3 kobolds trapped with 2 **Ochre Jellies**
- Running in circles screaming
- Jellies are slow but splitting when damaged
- Kobolds have "safety manual" that might actually help

**Section 3: The Crusher Walls**
- 4 kobolds in corridor with slowly closing walls
- They're trying to prop them open with "safety poles"
- Mechanical/strength challenge to jam mechanism
- One kobold taking notes: "Very unsafe! Must document!"

**Section 4: The Philosophy Circle**
- 5 kobolds trapped with magical mouth asking riddles
- Wrong answers trigger lightning bolts
- Riddles are about Order vs Chaos
- Kobolds keep arguing instead of answering

**Section 5: The Control Center**
- 2 kobolds (including Yip) at ancient control panel
- Pressing random buttons making things worse
- Need Translation/Arcana to read Dwarvish/Elvish labels
- Each button affects different sections

### The Challenge Layers

**Layer 1: Rescue Operations** (Simultaneous crises)
- Party must split up or prioritize
- Each round without help, situations worsen:
  - Water rises 1 foot
  - Jellies move toward kobolds
  - Walls close 5 feet
  - Lightning bolts fire
  - Random buttons pressed

**Layer 2: The Kobold Debate** (Ongoing during rescue)
Kobolds won't stop arguing about Order vs Chaos even while drowning:
- "This trap is Order! Predictable patterns!"
- "No, is Chaos! We triggered randomly!"
- Party's explanations influence their Contest stance

**Layer 3: The System Puzzle**
**Control Panel** has six buttons with worn symbols:
1. Hammer (Order) - Stabilizes one section
2. Lightning (Chaos) - Randomizes barriers
3. Water - Controls flooding
4. Fire - Activates/deactivates hazards
5. Eye - Shows different sections
6. Hand - Manual override (requires password)

**The Password Challenge:**
- Found in three parts throughout sections
- Written in different languages (Dwarvish, Elvish, Draconic)
- Relates to the original Forge of Spells
- Kobolds have mistranslated hilariously

### The Escalation: The Guardian Awakens
**When half the kobolds are rescued:**
- Ancient **Shield Guardian** activates
- Programmed to "maintain containment"
- Sees everyone as intruders
- AC 17, HP 142, tries to reset all traps

**Combat Complications:**
- Fighting damages control systems
- Some kobolds want to help, others hide
- Guardian repairs itself at control panel
- Can be deactivated with proper commands

### The Resolution Paths

**Path 1: Technical Solution**
- Decode control panel properly
- Use password to gain manual control
- Shut down systematically
- Kobolds impressed by Order approach

**Path 2: Chaos Solution**  
- Overload system with contradictions
- Press all buttons simultaneously
- Causes shutdown but damages cave
- Kobolds love the chaotic creativity

**Path 3: Kobold Empowerment**
- Teach kobolds to solve their own problems
- Guide them through rescue operations
- They gain confidence and competence
- Become effective delegation

### The Aftermath: Kobold Loyalty

**Grateful Kobolds Share Information:**
- Detailed (if confused) Contest explanation
- Map of all delegation gathering points
- Warning: "Shadow peoples in Phandalin. Very suspicious!"
- Offer to be party's "safety scouts"

**Yip's Revelation:**
"You saved us with [Order/Chaos/Both]! We understand now! At Contest, we argue for [whatever party demonstrated]!"

**The Dragon Question:**
- Kobolds reveal they're trying to represent dragons
- No true dragon has appeared yet
- They have a lead: "Old blue dragon in eastern mountains might come if convinced"
- Party could help them recruit actual dragon

### Rewards Based on Approach

**If Saved with Order:**
- **Kobold Safety Manual** (advantage on trap detection)
- Kobolds become Order delegation
- Organized kobold support network

**If Saved with Chaos:**
- **Experimental Kobold Devices** (random magical effects)
- Kobolds become Chaos delegation  
- Chaotic but enthusiastic allies

**If Saved with Balance:**
- Both rewards
- Kobolds can't decide stance
- Will support party's recommendation at Contest

### Long-term Consequences
- 18 kobold lives saved or lost
- Their Contest stance influenced
- Possible recurring allies/complications
- Knowledge of ancient cave systems
- Guardian might be reprogrammed as ally
