## Card Format Guide
Each card contains:
- **Delegation Name & Philosophy** (Order/Chaos/Undecided)
- **First Contact** - How PCs first encounter them
- **Key NPCs** - 2-3 memorable characters with quirks
- **Cultural Quirks** - Unique behaviors/customs
- **Tension Points** - What creates conflict
- **Potential Hooks** - 2-3 immediate adventure seeds
- **Resources** - What they offer/need
- **Combat Stats** - Quick reference if needed

## 1. THE SILVERED CHAIN (Githyanki)
**Philosophy**: ORDER (but internally divided)
**First Contact**: Dawn patrol spots silver-skinned warriors who immediately bow and retreat when seen

### Key NPCs
- **Zetch'r'r the Humbled** (Leader) - Former raider-captain, speaks in whispers, flinches at raised voices. Wears simple robes over ceremonial armor she can't bear to abandon. Has nervous habit of touching empty sword-belt where her silver sword once hung.
- **Yath'ki the Doubter** (Lieutenant) - Young githyanki born during the civil wars, questions everything, secretly thinks they need Chaos. Constantly sketches in a journal, documenting "surface oddities."
- **Mor'az One-Hand** (Elder Knight) - Lost hand in their third civil war, bitter but loyal. Makes dark jokes about their people's failures. "At least we're consistent in our incompetence."

### Cultural Quirks
- Leave payment for anything they might have disturbed
- Refuse to enter buildings without permission (even public ones)
- Meditate at dawn and dusk facing different directions
- Refer to themselves in past tense when discussing achievements

### Tension Points
- Half the delegation secretly favors Chaos
- Other delegations remember their past raids
- Paranoid about illithid influence
- Younger githyanki don't understand the shame

### Potential Hooks
- **The Deserter**: A young githyanki flees to the party, claims Zetch'r'r is too weak to lead
- **Silver Sword Fragment**: They seek pieces of a shattered silver sword as proof of redemption
- **Astral Stalkers**: Githyanki loyalists from the Astral Plane hunt the "traitors"

### Resources
**Offers**: Astral silk (plane-shifting properties), knowledge of planar threats
**Needs**: Acceptance, proof of redemption, protection from assassins

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Zetch'r'r**: Fighter 8, AC 16, HP 72, Multiattack, Misty Step
- **Knights (6)**: Fighter 4, AC 15, HP 32, Silver sword techniques (no swords)

## 2. THE CANDLEKEEPER COLLECTIVE (Kobolds)
**Philosophy**: CHAOS (but think they're bringing Order through "safety")
**First Contact**: Party wakes to find their inn room "trap-proofed" - door won't open, windows sealed, chickens stationed as "alarms"

### Key NPCs
- **High Trapmaster Skrix the Overprepared** - Wears 47 pouches, each labeled. Constantly updating a massive scroll of "safety protocols." Believes blinking too fast is suspicious. Has a pet chicken named "Emergency Protocol Seven."
- **Deputy Candlekeeper Yip** - In charge of the bucket brigade. Practices fire drills at 3 AM. Has memorized 1,847 types of "potential fire hazards" including "suspicious breathing."
- **Sacred Scribe Tiktak** - Takes notes on EVERYTHING. Has theories about how often people should breathe to maintain "optimal peace." Measures distances between people's feet while they talk.

### Cultural Quirks
- Form bucket brigades randomly (no fire needed)
- Install tiny bells on literally everything
- Believe all surface customs are elaborate tests
- Use chickens as universal solution to problems
- Conduct "safety inspections" at inappropriate times
- Create evacuation plans for impossible scenarios

### Tension Points
- Their "help" makes daily life impossible
- Don't understand why people reject safety measures
- Convinced other delegations are "in on the trap"
- The 44 uninvited cousins cause chaos

### Potential Hooks
- **The Great Chicken Escape**: Their safety chickens form their own delegation
- **The Midnight Improvement**: Kobolds "fix" something critical, break everything
- **The Real Trap**: They accidentally discover an actual threat while paranoid about fake ones

### Resources
**Offers**: Excessive rope, thousands of candles, questionable "safety improvements"
**Needs**: Validation, inclusion, someone to take their theories seriously

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Skrix**: Rogue 5, AC 15, HP 27, Trap expertise, endless pouches
- **Kobold Squad (47)**: AC 12, HP 5, Pack Tactics, Sling/Dagger

## 3. THE BROKEN TUSK CONFEDERATION (Orcs)
**Philosophy**: ORDER (surprising everyone)
**First Contact**: Formal embassy arrives with gift-giving ceremony, orcs reciting ancient hospitality laws in perfect Common

### Key NPCs
- **Chieftain Mokran the Poet** - Massive orc who speaks entirely in verse. Writes epic poems about mundane events. "The kettle sings its steaming song / The tea reminds us we belong." Carries a leather-bound book instead of weapons.
- **Shaman Yazga Dreamwalker** - Elderly female orc, blind, "sees" through spiritual sight. Laughs at inappropriate times because she sees the "cosmic joke." Makes uncomfortably accurate predictions.
- **Gorthak the Gentle** - Young orc warrior who refuses to fight. Tends wounded animals. Other orcs mock him until they need healing. Speaks to horses, claims they give good advice.

### Cultural Quirks
- Insist on formal gift exchange before any conversation
- Document everything in song form
- Young orcs practice "aggressive hospitality"
- View refusing their food as declaration of war
- Compete through poetry battles instead of combat

### Tension Points
- Traditional orcs arrive to challenge their "weakness"
- Other delegations don't trust the change
- One violent incident could ruin everything
- Their earnestness makes people suspicious

### Potential Hooks
- **The Honor Duel**: Traditional orcs challenge Mokran; he insists on poetry battle
- **The Lost Verses**: Ancient orc peacesongs were stolen; need recovery
- **The Feast Incident**: Someone poisons the peace feast to frame orcs

### Resources
**Offers**: Preserved foods, leather goods, oral histories, unexpected wisdom
**Needs**: Respect, chance to prove change, protection from traditionalists

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Mokran**: Bard 7, AC 14, HP 52, Inspiration, Counterspell through poetry
- **Warriors (20)**: AC 13, HP 15, Multiattack, but prefer not to fight

## 4. HOUSE MEL'THIIR (Reformed Drow)
**Philosophy**: ORDER (fleeing Chaos of Lolth)
**First Contact**: Mysterious gifts appear at night - healing fungi left at the temple, spider silk bandages at the clinic

### Key NPCs
- **Priestess Velara Mel'thiir** - Serene surface, constant vigilance. Dances to Eilistraee under moonlight. Wears Lolth's holy symbol inverted as reminder. Flinches at spider imagery but keeps pet jumping spider named "Hope."
- **Zilzar the Twice-Burned** - Male drow warrior with acid scars. Escaped Lolth twice. Makes self-deprecating jokes about trust. "I wouldn't trust me either, but here we are."
- **Child-Priestess Lilae** - 40 years old (child for elf), born on surface. Innocently asks why surface dwellers fear the dark. Sees in darkness better than light, bumps into things during day.

### Cultural Quirks
- Only travel/meet at night
- Sing hymns to Eilistraee at moonrise
- Apologize preemptively for their race's reputation
- Gift-giving includes warning labels about drow items
- Cover all spider imagery they encounter

### Tension Points
- Lolth loyalists hunt them constantly
- Surface dwellers instinctively distrust
- Dwarves have ancestral hatred
- Sunlight causes them physical pain

### Potential Hooks
- **The Assassin's Strike**: Lolth cultists attack during crucial negotiations
- **The Redemption Dance**: Velara must perform sacred dance, needs protection
- **The Fungal Garden**: Their healing fungi garden is sabotaged

### Resources
**Offers**: Healing fungi, spider silk, Underdark intelligence, poison antidotes
**Needs**: Safe haven, acceptance, protection from Lolth's agents

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Velara**: Cleric 9, AC 15, HP 58, Healing Word, Dancing Lights
- **Guards (8)**: AC 15, HP 26, Dual wielding, Faerie Fire

## 5. THE ELDEROAK PARLIAMENT (Nature Spirits)
**Philosophy**: UNDECIDED (Order vs Chaos debate)
**First Contact**: Party finds all plants in area leaning toward town center, animals gathering in mixed species groups

### Key NPCs
- **Speaker Mosswhisper** - Ancient awakened oak, speaks slowly, roots visible when walking. Refers to everything in tree-time: "Just yesterday (50 years ago)..." Has romantic tension with local regular oak.
- **Willowmend the Gardener** - Elf druid for Order. Obsessively prunes everything. Carries tiny scissors. Believes nature should be "curated for optimal beauty." Argues with every weed.
- **Bloodmoon** - Awakened dire wolf for Chaos. Philosophical about eating. "Death feeds life, life feeds death, it's all quite beautiful really." Vegetarian except during full moons.
- **Chorus of Sparrows** - Twelve awakened sparrows who share one consciousness. Finish each other's sentences. Constantly gossip about other delegations. "Did you see—" "—what the kobolds—" "—did to that door?"

### Cultural Quirks
- Vote on everything through root network
- Take literally hours to make decisions
- Constantly photosynthesize during meetings
- Refer to buildings as "dead trees"
- Pollinate instead of shaking hands

### Tension Points
- Can't agree if nature means harmony or competition
- Urban delegations don't understand their needs
- Awakened predators vs prey create tension
- Move and think too slowly for urgent decisions

### Potential Hooks
- **The Great Sprouting**: Buildings start growing leaves, need natural balance restored
- **Predator's Hunger**: Bloodmoon's pack grows restless, threatens peace
- **The Blighted Root**: Someone poisons Mosswhisper's roots

### Resources
**Offers**: Natural remedies, weather prediction, grown shelters, fertile soil
**Needs**: Protected groves, respect for plant consciousness, time (lots of it)

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Mosswhisper**: AC 16 (bark), HP 138, Entangle, Animated trees
- **Druids (3)**: Level 5, AC 14, HP 38, Wild Shape, Entangle

## 6. THE ROADHEARTS (Refugees/Wanderers)
**Philosophy**: TRUE NEUTRAL (Balance through diversity)
**First Contact**: Caravan of 30+ wagons arrives, each flying different cultural flags, cooking smells from dozen cuisines

### Key NPCs
- **Grandmother Safa** - Appears as different ages to different people. Knows everyone's grandmother's recipes. Speaks in proverbs that are irritatingly relevant. Has "adopted" half the delegation. "Eat first, argue later."
- **Keras the Mapper** - Tiefling who charts roads that don't exist yet. Claims to map "future paths." His maps sometimes prove prophetic. Allergic to staying still.
- **The Unnamed Child** - Mysterious orphan who speaks only in questions. Different people hear different languages. Might be touched by divine. "Why do homes have walls?" "When does running become arriving?"

### Cultural Quirks
- Share food before any discussion
- Build temporary shrines to dozens of gods
- Each wagon follows different cultural rules
- Children belong to everyone and no one
- Tell stories instead of history

### Tension Points
- No unified leadership structure
- Some members are fleeing crimes
- Bring diseases from distant lands
- Traditional peoples see them as rootless

### Potential Hooks
- **The Scattered Treasure**: Each wagon carries piece of ancient artifact
- **The Pursuing Past**: Bounty hunters arrive seeking refugees
- **The Road's End**: Safa declares this might be journey's end, causing panic

### Resources
**Offers**: Exotic goods, distant news, unusual solutions, cultural bridges
**Needs**: Temporary shelter, medical aid, acceptance, safe passage

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Safa**: Stats change based on need, usually non-combatant
- **Guards (various)**: Mixed classes, levels 2-5, desperate but determined

## 7. CLAN TRUTHFORGE (Traditional Dwarves)
**Philosophy**: Split between ORDER (elders) and CHAOS (youth)
**First Contact**: Precisely at dawn, formal procession with traditional horns, ceremony for "measuring the sacred angles"

### Key NPCs
- **Elder Thane Grimvar Ironfoot** - 400 years old, beard to his knees, believes in tradition. Every statement starts with "In my day..." Literally turns to stone when extremely angry (reversible). Has a rivalry with his own beard.
- **Master Artificer Thorina Goldspeak** - 50 years old (young for delegation), innovative. Invented "improvements" to every traditional tool. Her mechanical familiar is a beer stein that refills itself. Says "Why?" to every tradition.
- **Battlepriest Durin the Doubtful** - Questions his faith publicly. "Moradin's hammer strikes true, but does it strike fairly?" Drunk philosopher. Gives sermons that trail into existential crisis.

### Cultural Quirks
- Measure everything three times
- Argue about pronunciation of ancient words
- Compete through beard-braiding complexity
- Consider rushed decisions (less than a week) heretical
- Toast before every conversation

### Tension Points
- Generational divide threatens delegation unity
- Both sides claim to be "true" delegation
- Artificer innovations offend traditionalists
- Takes forever to make any decision

### Potential Hooks
- **The Broken Covenant**: Ancient treaty requires unified dwarven voice
- **The Stolen Blueprints**: Traditional ceremonies plans go missing
- **The Beard Contest**: Delegation leadership decided by beard competition

### Resources
**Offers**: Masterwork items, stone-cunning, ancient protocols, endless ale
**Needs**: Internal unity, recognition of both traditions, patience from others

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Grimvar**: Fighter 10, AC 18, HP 94, Adamantine armor
- **Thorina**: Artificer 7, AC 15, HP 49, Infusions, Steel Defender

## 8. THE STARLIGHT CHORUS (Elves)
**Philosophy**: ORDER (through eternal preservation)
**First Contact**: Haunting songs in Elvish drift through town at sunset, no visible singers

### Key NPCs
- **Keeper Silviana Moonbrook** - Ancient elf who was child at last Remembrance. Occasionally forgets which century it is. Refers to humans by their great-grandparents' names. Cries at random when remembering dead friends.
- **Bladesinger Althorian** - Young elf (only 200) who thinks ancients are too cautious. Performs sword dances to "modern" music (only 100 years old). Has a crush on multiple delegates simultaneously.
- **Memory-Weaver Yaellan** - Records everything in song form. Cannot speak, only sings. Others must sing to her for responses. Gets frustrated when people don't know the harmony parts.

### Cultural Quirks
- Sing memories instead of writing them
- Take 17-hour meditation breaks
- Consider anything under 10 years "rushed"
- Bring saplings as diplomatic gifts
- Refuse to use furniture made from wood

### Tension Points
- Move too slowly for urgent threats
- Condescending about "younger" races
- Their songs contain uncomfortable truths
- Traditional enemies of drow delegation

### Potential Hooks
- **The Forgotten Verse**: Key part of peace song is missing
- **The Wilting Sapling**: Their Peace Tree cutting is dying
- **The Memory Thief**: Someone steals Yaellan's voice

### Resources
**Offers**: Ancient wisdom, Peace Tree saplings, prophetic songs, starlight wine
**Needs**: Patience, respect for traditions, protection of sacred groves

### Combat Quick Stats
- **Silviana**: Wizard 12, AC 15, HP 66, Time Stop, Counterspell
- **Singer-Warriors (6)**: AC 17, HP 44, Bladesong, Misty Step

## ENCOUNTER COMPLICATIONS TABLE

Roll d20 when delegations meet:

1-2: **Language Barrier** - Critical translation error causes offense
3-4: **Cultural Faux Pas** - Innocent gesture is deadly insult
5-6: **Mistaken Identity** - Delegation confused for enemies
7-8: **Resource Competition** - Both need same inn/shrine/grove
9-10: **Romantic Entanglement** - Delegates fall inappropriately in love
11-12: **Ancient Grudge** - Families have blood feud
13-14: **Saboteur Strike** - False flag attack during meeting
15-16: **Divine Intervention** - Someone's god directly objects
17-18: **Comedy of Errors** - Kobold "safety measures" trigger cascade
19: **Magical Mishap** - Truthstone resonance causes wild magic
20: **Moment of Unity** - Unexpected cooperation against shared threat

## DM QUICK TIPS

1. **Kobold Chaos**: Use for comedy relief when tension too high
2. **Githyanki Shame**: Perfect for redemption arcs
3. **Orc Poetry**: Describe everything in terrible verse
4. **Drow Paranoia**: Always checking for assassins
5. **Nature Time**: Count to 10 before trees respond
6. **Refugee Diversity**: Different accent for each wagon
7. **Dwarf Arguments**: Everything requires committee
8. **Elf Condescension**: Accidentally insult everyone's age

Remember: Every delegation believes they're the heroes of this story!
