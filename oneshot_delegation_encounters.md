# One-Shot Delegation Encounters

## Format for Each Encounter
Each delegation encounter is designed for 2-3 hours of play, complete with:
- Initial hook that drops into ongoing campaign
- Clear Order vs Chaos philosophical conflict
- Combat option (if negotiations fail)
- Social/puzzle resolution preferred
- Consequences that matter for the Contest

---

## 1. The Dwarven Schism
**Philosophy:** Order (tradition) vs Chaos (innovation)
**Level:** 3-5

### The Setup
The party encounters two dwarven camps on opposite sides of the road to Phandalin, glaring at each other. Both claim to be the "true" dwarven delegation.

**Camp Order:** Led by **Elder Thane Grimvar Ironfoot**
- 200-year-old traditionalist, every beard braid perfect
- Brings ancient ceremonial hammers used in the last five Contests
- "The old ways ARE the mountain's strength"
- Demands the party help them prevent "radical corruption"

**Camp Chaos:** Led by **Master Artificer Thorina Goldspeak** 
- 50-year-old inventor with gunpowder-singed beard
- Brings revolutionary magical innovations
- "Stagnation is death by comfort"
- Asks party to help them prove innovation honors ancestors

### The Conflict
- Both camps are sabotaging each other's preparations
- Order camp: stealing Thorina's experimental devices
- Chaos camp: replacing traditional items with "improvements"
- A sacred anvil has gone missing - each blames the other

### The Truth
**Kobolds** (the Candlekeeper Collective) actually stole the anvil, thinking it was a trap component. They're "protecting" both camps from this "dangerous device."

### Resolution Options
- **Investigate:** Find kobold tracks, follow to elaborate "safe house" full of confused chickens
- **Mediate:** Convince both camps they need each other (tradition guides innovation)
- **Combat:** Fight awakened constructs if players bungle negotiations
- **Creative:** Help forge a NEW ceremonial item combining both philosophies

### Consequences
- United delegation = strong Order OR Chaos voice (player choice influences which)
- Divided delegation = weakened dwarf influence in Contest
- Kobolds might become comic relief allies

## 2. The Wild Hunt's Dilemma
**Philosophy:** Natural selection (Chaos) vs Protection of weak (Order)
**Level:** 4-6

### The Setup
Screams from the forest! The party finds a dryad and a dire wolf in heated argument over a wounded deer. Both claim to represent nature in the Contest.

**The Order Voice:** **Willowmend**, a dryad healer
- Leads the **Shepherds of the Grove** (druids who protect)
- "Nature nurtures all its children"
- The wounded deer is pregnant with twins
- Wants to heal it, breaking natural selection

**The Chaos Voice:** **Bloodmoon**, an awakened dire wolf
- Leads the **Truth of Fangs** (predators and harsh-season druids)
- "The weak must fall for the strong to rise"
- The deer would feed his starving pack
- Survival of the fittest is nature's wisdom

### The Escalation
- Other forest creatures are choosing sides
- Pixies vs ravens, bears vs badgers
- The forest itself is responding - thorns grow thicker, paths shift
- Both claim the party must help them "prove nature's truth"

### The Real Problem
The deer was wounded by **poachers' traps** - neither natural predation nor natural healing. Human interference created this dilemma.

### Resolution Options
- **Investigation:** Find the poachers, reveal external threat
- **Philosophy:** Argue that both philosophies fail against unnatural threats
- **Compromise:** Heal deer but mark it for future natural selection
- **Combat:** If failed, face summoned nature spirits from both sides

### Mini-Skill Challenge
**Convincing the Wild**: 
- Animal Handling to calm tensions
- Nature to speak their language
- Persuasion/Intimidation for philosophical arguments
- Medicine to actually help the deer

### Consequences
- United = Nature speaks with powerful voice at Contest
- Divided = Two separate nature delegations, weakening both
- Poacher threat might become future adventure

## 3. The Githyanki Test
**Philosophy:** Redemption through order vs Freedom through chaos
**Level:** 5-7

### The Setup
A silver-skinned Githyanki warrior stands at Phandalin's gates at dawn, unmoving. She's been there three days. Guards are terrified but she hasn't drawn her sword.

**The Penitent:** **Zetch'r'r the Humbled**
- Missed the last Contest, her people suffered 147 years of civil war
- Now totally committed to Order: "Structure prevents suffering"
- But her remaining followers are fracturing

### The Crisis
Her six followers are splitting:
- Three want to embrace Chaos: "Freedom from the Lich Queen's tyranny!"
- Three support Order: "Discipline kept us from extinction!"
- Tonight they duel at midnight to settle it
- Zetch'r'r is paralyzed - choosing sides breaks her delegation

### The Complication
**Young githyanki rebels** arrive, hunting Zetch'r'r as a traitor. They serve neither Order nor Chaos, just the Lich Queen. The party must handle two crises:
1. Prevent the philosophical duel
2. Protect the delegation from assassination

### Combat Encounter (if needed)
- **Githyanki Knight** + 4 **Githyanki Warriors** (rebels)
- Zetch'r'r's followers might help OR fight each other
- Environmental hazard: psychic storms from emotional conflict

### Resolution Options
- **Unite against external threat:** Rebels force cooperation
- **Philosophical synthesis:** Both philosophies needed for redemption
- **Individual choice:** Let each Githyanki choose their own path
- **Trial by combat:** Party champions one philosophy

### Consequences
- Githyanki delegation's stance influences other "redeemed" peoples
- Zetch'r'r might become recurring ally/mentor
- Lich Queen attention on region

## 4. The Dragon's Proxy
**Philosophy:** Might makes right (Chaos) vs Responsibility of power (Order)
**Level:** 6-8

### The Setup
A brass wyrmling named **Chattertooth** has declared himself dragon delegate, setting up "court" in the town square. He's demanding tribute, telling stories, and genuinely having the time of his life.

### The Problem
Three other candidates arrived:
- **Scarred dragonborn paladin** (Order): "Dragons must protect the weak"
- **Half-dragon sorcerer** (Chaos): "Dragon blood is about freedom and power"
- **Kobold colony** claiming their whole tribe equals one dragon (wildcard)

Each claims Chattertooth is too young/stupid/small to represent dragonkind.

### The Test
Chattertooth proposes a traditional dragon challenge: **The Three Trials**
1. **Riddles** (Intelligence) - But Chattertooth asks about human pop culture
2. **Strength** (Combat) - But against solving practical town problems
3. **Hoard** (Wealth) - But measuring friends, not gold

### The Twist
An **ancient blue dragon's projection** appears - **Othokent the Sapphire** is watching! He's testing whether any understand what dragons REALLY need to learn. (Hint: it's humility and connection, not power)

### Resolution Options
- **Help Chattertooth:** Teach him responsibility while preserving his joy
- **Support another:** Each candidate offers different Contest influence
- **Unite them all:** Form a "Council of Scales" delegation
- **Combat:** If negotiations fail, face dragon-blooded rivals

### Consequences
- Dragon stance at Contest depends on solution
- Othokent might reveal himself based on party choices
- Chattertooth becomes beloved or bitter NPC

## 5. The Merchant's Gambit
**Philosophy:** Free trade (Chaos) vs Regulated commerce (Order)
**Level:** 3-5

### The Setup
Every merchant in Phandalin has gone on strike simultaneously. The **Roadhearts** delegation (wandering traders) and the **Guild of Counted Coins** (established merchants) are in economic warfare.

**Grandmother Safa** (Roadhearts/Chaos): 
- "Trade flows like water, finding its own level"
- Giving away free goods to prove abundance
- Creating a gift economy

**Master Goldwright** (Guild/Order):
- "Fair prices protect both buyer and seller"
- Has locked all permanent shops
- Demanding standardized delegation taxes

### The Escalation
- Townspeople are choosing sides based on immediate needs
- Food is running out (or being hoarded?)
- Both sides ask party to investigate the other for "sabotage"

### The Discovery
The Twin Prophets themselves are in town, disguised as:
- **Serenity**: A baker giving free bread
- **Strife**: A weapons merchant price-gouging
They're testing how the town handles economic pressure

### Resolution
- **Recognize the prophets**: Wisdom/Insight checks reveal divine nature
- **Create compromise**: Temporary economy serving immediate needs
- **Social encounter**: Convince prophets the town learned their lesson
- **Market mini-game**: Players actually run negotiations between merchants

### Consequences
- Economic philosophy influences Contest
- Prophets might offer cryptic guidance
- Town's economy permanently changed

## Running These Encounters

### Starting Hooks
- "You see unusual travelers on the road..."
- "The town guard asks for help with 'diplomatic incidents'"
- "Daran Edermath specifically requests your aid"
- "Strange dreams lead you to witness..."

### Scaling Difficulty
- **Lower level**: Focus on social/investigation
- **Higher level**: Add combat complications
- **Mixed party**: Multiple resolution paths

### Continuity Options
- Each resolved delegation mentions others
- Collect "Delegation Tokens" proving involvement
- Build toward final Contest participation
- NPCs from each encounter appear at finale

### The Bigger Picture
After 3-4 delegation encounters, party realizes:
- The Contest is REAL and IMMINENT
- Their choices are shaping delegations' arguments
- Order currently has slight advantage (recent chaos)
- Something bigger might be manipulating events
